HSN-Shaders Lite 26.3.1
Low-end pack for Iris + Fabric (Minecraft 26.3).

What it does
- Hard/soft shadows (512 default)
- Bloom (toggle in Bloom / Glow — works on every profile)
- Water screen-space reflections (on in High; turn on by hand in Low/Medium)
- Vanilla sun and moon sprites (no drawn circle)
- Simple color / fog / water tint
- Waving plants (optional)
- Extra toggles from the older menu: god rays, 2D clouds, AO, VL

What it does NOT do (on purpose)
- No heavy RenderPearl PBR / light-list
- No custom sun disc

Profiles
- Potato: no shadows, no bloom, no reflections
- Low: 512 shadows + bloom
- Medium: 1024 shadows + bloom
- High: soft 1024 shadows + bloom + water reflections + god rays

Install: drop the zip in shaderpacks. Do not unzip.

Lighting adapted from Miniature Shader (MIT, Mateus A. Kreuch).
Reflections are a small screen-space trace in the same spirit as RenderPearl, written for this lite pack.
