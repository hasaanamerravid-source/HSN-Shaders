HSN-Shaders Lite 26.3.7
A lightweight shader pack for Minecraft 26.3

HSN-Shaders is a performance-focused shader pack designed for Iris and OptiFine. It adds subtle visual enhancements while remaining lightweight and suitable for a wide range of hardware.

Compatible with Minecraft 26.3 (Iris recommended; OptiFine builds that support the same shader format also work).

Features
- Performance profiles: Potato, Low, Medium, and High
- Configurable soft and hard shadows with adjustable distance, bias, brightness, and distortion
- Bloom and highlight glow on bright sources (torches, lanterns, lava, portals, soul fire, sun, and moon)
- Post-processing controls: bloom, vignette, sharpen, chromatic aberration, exposure, warmth, saturation, and contrast
- Atmosphere: daytime and nighttime fog with density and strength controls; rain-aware sky
- Water: optional surface waves; water remains close to the vanilla look
- Foliage: waving grass, flowers, crops, and leaves (vines remain static)
- Experimental options (disabled by default): screen-space ambient occlusion (SSAO) and god rays

Profiles (approximate)
- Potato: maximum performance — soft shadows, no bloom, no fog, no waving plants
- Low: light enhancements — soft shadows, modest bloom and fog
- Medium: balanced look — soft shadows, bloom, fog, and color grading (default)
- High: extended quality — longer shadow distance and stronger bloom while keeping the same overall style

Installation
1. Install Iris (recommended) or a compatible OptiFine build for Minecraft 26.3.
2. Place the HSN-Shaders zip file directly into your .minecraft/shaderpacks folder.
3. Do not unzip the file.
4. In Minecraft, go to Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs and select HSN-Shaders.

Configuration
Open the shader menu in-game to choose a profile or adjust individual settings. Profiles can be further customized. Experimental features are available on their own menu page and are turned off by default.
